# Workstation Examples

Sixteen filled-in workstation examples. Each one shows what a working CLAUDE.md and MEMORY.md looks like after personalisation. Use them as starting points; replace the content with your own.

## How to use these

1. Copy the folder for the workstation you want into your OS root.
2. Rename the folder if needed (keep it lowercase with hyphens).
3. Replace the placeholder content with your own specifics.
4. Add a row to your root CLAUDE.md Routing Map.

You do not have to use all of them. Start with one or two that match your immediate needs. Add more as you find domains where Claude keeps losing context.

## The difference between a template and an example

Templates (in `templates/`) have `{{REPLACE: ...}}` markers. They are blank forms.

Examples (here) have placeholder content written in. They show what a real workstation looks like at setup. They are starting points, not finished products.

Replace the example content with your own. The structure stays the same.
